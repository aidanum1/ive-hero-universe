# Research notes — IVE "Looks Can Kill" (GIRL HERO) era

Visual research only. Images in `references/promo/` are official promotional material
(© Starship Entertainment) saved for study; they are **not** used in the site or videos.
This folder sits outside `/public` on purpose so the photos are never served, and
`references/promo/` is git-ignored. `/public/references` is kept for any references you
add yourself.

## Facts used in the project
- Release: 5th mini album **"Looks Can Kill"** — EP out **26 Oct 2026, 18:00 KST**.
  Pre-release title track + MV: 19 Oct 2026, 18:00 KST.
- Tracklist: 1 Looks Can Kill · 2 Mirror Mirror · 3 SNAKESKIN · 4 CRUMBS · 5 Girls Girls Girls · 6 Glow Motion.
- Concept: **GIRL HERO** — "anyone can be the main character and hero of their own life".
  Promo phases: POST CREDIT SCENE: GIRL HERO (teaser film, 6 Oct) → GIRL HERO photos →
  HERO TRAINING CENTER / "Hero Academy" → HERO COMICS (12–14 Oct) → MV teasers → HERO ON SET.
- Hero identities: Yujin = **Captain Purple**, Gaeul = **Wonder Green**, Rei = **Jean Yellow**,
  Wonyoung = **Bat Pink**, Liz = **Super Blue**, Leeseo = **Spider Red**.
- Training-centre powers: Yujin fast impactful landings / lightning (MV); Gaeul wind waves & palm
  blast; Rei finger-gun shots, bandaged fists; Wonyoung staff/pole strikes; Liz precision fork
  throws at a target; Leeseo long hair-rope, yo-yo.
- Yujin's "teleport" move went viral after the 19 Oct stage ("Quicksilver Yujin", "Glitchin",
  #QUICKJINCHALLENGE) — inspiration for the YUJIN GLITCH sequence (built graphically, no footage).
- MV grammar: superhero film that keeps stepping off set — the girls as stars hired to play heroes.

## Visual principles extracted (not copied)
- Signature colour per member, worn as accents on black, or set against pastel worlds.
- Glossy translucent candy masks, opera gloves, satin pastels: fashion first, not muscle-hero.
- Comic collage: tilted panels, thick black keylines, halftone fills in member colour,
  picture-in-picture circle/oval insets, star bursts at impact points.
- Heavy italic outlined lettering with offset shadow for SFX words.
- Kitsch "monogram" wallpaper of tiny symbols.
- Retro-game kitsch; high contrast.
